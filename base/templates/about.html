The Craft of Donzel

Sub-Zero Churned Gelato & Artisanal Cold Cocoa Boutique

Sub-Zero Philosophy

Pure Dairy, Zero Compromise

Donzel Artisanal Ice Cream Parlour was created to elevate frozen desserts into an exquisite culinary experience. We reject palm oil, vegetable fats, and artificial powders.

Our ice creams and gelatos are slow-churned in small batches using 100% pure A2 dairy cream, real Alphonso mango pulp, wild berries, and authentic 70% dark Belgian cocoa.

🥛

100% Pure A2 Dairy

Creamy, velvety texture achieved strictly through premium milk fats without any artificial thickeners.

🍫

Belgian Cocoa Beans

Our famous DND Cold Cocoa uses single-origin dark cocoa nibs for a deep, lingering chocolate bliss.

🌿

100% Eggless Recipes

Every single scoop, waffle cone, and fudgy brownie is 100% vegetarian and eggless.
